## Goal

Harden the cross-agent `/experiment` harness so every agent (claude, copilot, opencode, mastra) launches and is measured under **symmetric initial conditions** — each cell invoking its agent through the SAME proxy-routing/env/model seam as `bin/coding --<agent>` — with a per-agent pre-flight gate that records a clean `skip:<reason>` up front instead of a mid-run abort, and probe/ambient sessions that do not pollute the Runs view.

This closes the harness-correctness gaps that the v7.5 dogfood investigation exposed: opencode cells failing on a `rapid-proxy/` model-not-found, copilot `auto` cells aborting with a bare HTTP 500, and drivability-probe rows leaking into the experiment comparison.

**Lineage note:** the v7.6 slot was originally earmarked (in `v7.5-REQUIREMENTS.md`) for policy automation / auto-routing consuming the runner's comparisons. In practice, harness hardening surfaced as the higher-priority v7.6; the policy-automation work reslots to a later milestone.
